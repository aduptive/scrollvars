ScrollVars perf lab

Three reference pages, scrolled top to bottom on this device: a long institutional page, a deep-DOM page and a page of 42 cube-window shapes. About 3 to 5 minutes with the defaults. Open this on any browser you have, and on a phone over the same network.

  • Turn off Low Power Mode / battery saver: it caps the frame rate.
  • Close other apps and do not touch the screen until the table shows up.
  • Keep the tab in front: leaving it restarts the page it was on.
